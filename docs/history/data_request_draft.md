Asunto: Clarification of bilateral EMG export and original recordings for a methodological reproducibility study

Dear authors,

We are developing an exploratory methodological study of how explicit amplitude-summary choices affect bilateral vastus lateralis sEMG interpretation following ACL reconstruction. We have inspected version 2 of your public Figshare dataset (10.6084/m9.figshare.30928694.v2) and wish to avoid misinterpreting the exported data.

Could you clarify the processing and units of the two EMG workbooks, each containing 101 points per muscle and participant? In particular, are these signals envelopes, interpolated/averaged gait cycles, or another representation, and has amplitude been normalized in these files?

For independent methodological validation, would it be possible to obtain de-identified original bilateral recordings, together with sampling rates, filtering/rectification settings, gait-event or trial boundaries, normalization references, and an explicit mapping between signal sheet identifiers and participant identifiers? If available, separate repetitions and session identifiers would allow us to distinguish specification sensitivity from repeatability.

We also observed a K-STARTS worksheet but no separate ACL-RSI field. Could you clarify whether participant-level ACL-RSI is available and whether it may be linked to the signal identifiers?
