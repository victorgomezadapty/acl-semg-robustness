# Band-pass sensitivity and signal-validity audit

Generated by `scripts/evaluate_bandpass.py`. Retrospective analysis added on 2026-10-07 after review; not preregistered.

## Sampling rate

The authors do not state the EMG sampling rate for this export. Every one of the 88 exported clocks gives (samples − 1) / elapsed time between 1923.91 and 1925.93 Hz (median 1925.92 Hz). This matches the standard EMG rate of the Delsys Trigno system named in the related author paper and the dataset README. Clocks with many duplicate timestamps give the same rate, so the duplicates reflect clock rounding rather than missing samples. The rate is therefore estimated from the data, not assumed; it remains an estimate, and the exact Delsys preprocessing is still undocumented.

## Band-pass specification

A zero-phase 4th-order Butterworth band-pass of 20–450 Hz (a common surface-EMG band) was applied to each record and all 21 amplitude specifications were recomputed (1848 evaluations). It is an additional specification, not a replacement for the exported baseline.

## In-band energy as a quality indicator

In-band share is band-passed RMS divided by mean-centered RMS. Its median across recordings is 0.986. Records below 0.8: healthy9/iso90/uninv (0.62), healthy9/iso180/uninv (0.70), healthy8/iso90/uninv (0.71), healthy3/iso180/uninv (0.79). Low values indicate substantial drift or movement content outside the EMG band. The 0.8 cut-off is a retrospective review heuristic; flagged records stay in every analysis.

## healthy8 / iso90 pair validity

The healthy8 iso90 uninv record lasts 2.00 s; the next shortest of all 88 records lasts 10.5 s. It meets the existing short-record rule (below 25% of the cohort-condition median length), and 29% of its centered RMS lies outside the EMG band. A whole-record ratio between a ~25 s task and a ~2 s fragment does not compare like with like, so this pair is excluded from the primary presentation and retained as a sensitivity analysis. Its ratio is 25.2 exported and 64.6 band-passed. The rule and its promotion to an exclusion were decided retrospectively; the cause (truncated export, failed trial or acquisition issue) cannot be determined from the files.
