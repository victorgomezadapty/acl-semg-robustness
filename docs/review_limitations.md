# Limitations and attribution

No clinical outcome, MVC, torque or IMU is available. Control dominance and contraction boundaries are unresolved. Clocks include duplicates. Ratios do not eliminate unequal bilateral acquisition gain. Specifications are correlated choices, not independent observations.

Processing sensitivity has antecedents including [Benoit 2003](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0966-6362(02)00194-7). Our 44-record PubMed search is focused, not an exhaustive systematic review or proof of novelty. [Search audit](history/scientific_novelty_review.md).

Centering and presentation changes are retrospective. Simulation intensities are illustrative, not estimated physiological error distributions. Shared carriers simplify bilateral signals; spectral properties, motor-unit recruitment and fatigue are not modeled. Small samples and uncertain bootstrap coverage limit inference. No clinical clearance or calibrated reliability metric is supplied.

Contribution: dataset-specific transparency and reproducible controlled failure cases. Scientific priority and external/clinical validation are unestablished. Project tables, figures and code are locally generated; paper figures/text/code were not copied. [Source](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.66t1g1k4j) · [license distinctions](CODE_LICENSE.md).
