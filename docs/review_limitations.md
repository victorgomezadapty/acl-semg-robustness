# Limitations and attribution

No clinical outcome, MVC, torque or IMU is available. Control dominance and contraction boundaries are unresolved. Clocks include duplicates. The sampling rate is estimated from the clocks rather than stated by the authors, and the built-in Delsys preprocessing applied before export is undocumented, so the band-pass is a sensitivity analysis, not a reconstruction of the original processing. Ratios do not eliminate unequal bilateral acquisition gain. Specifications are correlated choices, not independent observations.

Processing sensitivity has antecedents including [Benoit 2003](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0966-6362(02)00194-7). Our 44-record PubMed search is focused, not an exhaustive systematic review or proof of novelty. [Search audit](history/scientific_novelty_review.md).

Centering, band-pass, the 0.8 RMS-retention review threshold and the exclusion of healthy8/iso90 were all decided retrospectively, after inspecting the data; each is reported alongside the unmodified baseline. The files cannot tell whether the 2 s healthy8 record reflects a truncated export, a failed trial or an acquisition problem. Simulation intensities are illustrative, not estimated physiological error distributions. Shared carriers simplify bilateral signals; spectral properties, motor-unit recruitment and fatigue are not modeled. Small samples and uncertain bootstrap coverage limit inference. No clinical clearance or calibrated reliability metric is supplied.

Contribution: dataset-specific transparency and reproducible controlled failure cases. Scientific priority and external/clinical validation are unestablished. Project tables, figures and code are locally generated; paper figures/text/code were not copied. [Source](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.66t1g1k4j) · [license distinctions](CODE_LICENSE.md).
